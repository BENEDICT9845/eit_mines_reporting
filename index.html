How it works

Reconcile → predict → decide

  1. 1

    Reconcile

    Reads the report by its disclosure codes, rebuilds each mine's water balance, and cross-checks it against WU Wien's satellite + machine-learning estimates. Output: one trusted number per mine.

  2. 2

    Predict

    Simulates 1,000 futures per mine to 2030: demand growth, droughts, tighter licences. Output: the chance of a shortfall and the revenue at risk.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Prices each fix (use less freshwater, secure a new source) against the risk it removes, and shows which one pays back first.

Why it gets harder to copy over time: every report added becomes a checked mine account that sharpens the estimates for mines that don't report. The asset is the reconciled dataset, not the file reader.
