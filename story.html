1 · The problem

Nobody can say how much water each mine uses.

Copper is the metal of the energy transition, and it runs on water. Mining companies report water as one company total, if at all. So buyers, banks, towns and researchers can't see which mine is at risk.

More copper, more water, and the rules are landing now.

Trend

The IEA expects a ~30% copper supply gap by 2035. Falling ore grades mean more rock and more water per tonne.

Climate

ICMM rates ~2/3 of mining facilities at high water risk. By 2034, ~2/3 of Chile's mining water is expected to come from the sea.

Funding

The EU Critical Raw Materials Act backs 47 strategic projects in the EU and 13 outside, and each one needs water data to get permits and finance.

4 · How it works

Parsing is step one. The engine decides which number to trust.

ingest.js

1 Read

Reads .xlsx and .csv in the browser with no library; tested on the Newcrest FY20 file. GRI 303 codes route each value to mine, flow, source and quality. Units in ML. Each value keeps its file, sheet and row. The company page import is a demo on the example files.

rules.js

2 Check

Water balance: withdrawal − discharge − Δstorage vs reported consumption. Over 5% is flagged. ICMM minimum checks and intensity per t ore, oz gold and US$m.

risk.js

3 Reconcile

Report, balance and WU Wien model, each with its own error bar, fused into one best estimate and a trust score.

risk.js

4 Predict

Seeded Monte Carlo, 1,000 futures per mine to 2030: demand, droughts, licence limits → chance of a short year, revenue at risk.

risk.js · sim.js

5 Decide

Prices fixes against the revenue they protect. sim.js runs reporting scenarios over all 447 WU Wien mines.

Try the reconciliation: Telfer, FY20

±35%
best = Σ(xᵢ / σᵢ²) / Σ(1 / σᵢ²)  ·  σ_best = 1 / √Σ(1 / σᵢ²)

Inverse-variance weighting: a precise source counts more. Inputs are real (report 18,159 ML; balance-implied 21,826 ML; WU Wien model 17,744 ML); error bars are assumptions.

What's unique

  • Provenance on every number. Each value links back to its file, sheet and row, and is labelled reported, balance-implied or modelled.
  • Reported and modelled data in one account. Research models and company reports check each other, instead of living in separate worlds.
  • Data quality becomes money. A verified Telfer balance cuts its bad-case loss from $315m to $148m. That's the value of better reporting.
  • Gets better with use. Every reconciled report is new real data for the model, so estimates for mines that don't report get tighter.

Main technical risks

Workbook layouts differ between companies → map by disclosure code, not row. Definitions differ (cooling seawater: Cobre Panama 113,321 vs 581,900 ML) → keep flows separate. Which model values are reported vs modelled → confirm with WU Wien.

6 · Who pays, and the pilot

Buyers and lenders pay for site-level water they can trust.

Price hypothesis: €900 per monitored mine per year, paid by buyers and lenders assumed

Free for mines. Time saved: a buyer's or lender's analyst goes from ~30 to ~4 hours per mine per year at €90/hour assumed. Market floor: ~12,000 ICMM sites × €900 ≈ €11m a year with one paying reader per site; every further buyer or bank adds to it.
